# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Packsmith is a developer tool for people building agent skills, prompts, and packaging workflows.

Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to keep discussion technical, respectful, and useful. Disagreement is fine. Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or deliberately hostile behavior are not.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent, but be precise.
- Critique ideas, code, docs, and decisions without demeaning the person behind them.
- Prefer evidence over status, volume, or dogma.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the repository's actual scope.
- Share reproduction steps, logs, diffs, or references when reporting problems.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, threats, or discrimination.
- Repeated personal attacks or bad-faith escalation.
- Deliberately derailing technical threads.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Using Packsmith spaces to spam, impersonate, or manipulate.

## Scope

This policy applies to GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, release threads, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a serious conduct problem, open a private security-style report through the contact path documented in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). If the issue is not sensitive, you can also open a normal issue so it is visible and actionable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, lock conversations, reject contributions, or block access when behavior clearly violates this policy or makes collaboration unsafe.

Enforcement decisions are made to protect the project and its contributors, not to win arguments.
