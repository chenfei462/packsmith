# Launch Researcher

Use this skill when preparing a product launch, validating a wedge, or turning raw demand signals into a plan.

## Trigger Conditions

- The user names this workflow directly.
- The task matches the pack description: Converts market signals into positioning, wedge, audience, and launch recommendations.
- The work would benefit from a repeatable agent process with clear evidence and output.

## Workflow

1. Collect evidence from repositories, issues, discussions, docs, competitors, and user language.
2. Separate durable demand from hype and unsupported assumptions.
3. Identify the narrow audience, urgent job, and first-session payoff.
4. Turn findings into positioning, launch channels, and proof points.
5. Recommend the smallest launch asset set needed to test demand.

## Output Format

Return:

- audience
- demand evidence
- positioning
- launch wedge
- next experiments

## Usage Example

```text
packsmith install launch-researcher
```

## Quality Bar

- Ground recommendations in files, commands, docs, or clearly labeled assumptions.
- Keep the response focused on the user request and avoid unrelated platform work.
- State what was not verified when evidence is incomplete.
