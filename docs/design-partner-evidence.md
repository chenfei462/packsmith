# Design Partner Evidence Capture

Packsmith needs real design-partner evidence before investor materials can claim external traction. Use this kit to interview 5-10 engineering teams that already use more than one AI coding agent and to capture qualitative evidence without inventing revenue, adoption, or named logos.

## Interview Script

Start with context: "Packsmith packages reusable AI-agent workflows with preview, policy, receipts, and audit before install. We are collecting evidence, not asking for a public endorsement today."

Ask:

1. Which coding agents or AI IDEs does your team use today?
2. Which review, test, security, documentation, release, or onboarding workflows get repeated across tools?
3. What breaks when those workflows are copied by hand?
4. Who is allowed to install or modify agent workflows on your team?
5. What evidence would make you trust a workflow before install?
6. Would policy, receipts, lockfiles, or audit reports change whether you could use shared workflows?
7. What would a successful pilot prove in one or two weeks?

## Pilot Evidence Checklist

- Team profile: size, engineering domain, agents used, and current workflow-sharing pattern.
- Before/After Workflow: capture the current manual process, then the Packsmith preview/install/audit process.
- Pack tested: name, version or commit, target runtime, command transcript, and date.
- Trust evidence: preview output, Passport output, policy result, receipt, lockfile, and audit result.
- Outcome: time saved, defects avoided, governance concern resolved, or blocker discovered; record the baseline source for any before/after number.
- Follow-up: whether the team wants another pilot, a private registry, policy controls, signing, or a Trae-native path if one becomes supported.

## Consent And Claiming Guardrails

- Consent: ask whether notes can be stored internally, quoted anonymously, quoted with attribution, used as a public reference, or deleted after the interview with no permission to quote.
- Do not publish names without permission.
- Do not claim revenue until money is collected.
- Do not claim production usage unless the team confirms production use.
- Do not claim npm downloads, GitHub adoption, or third-party packs without public evidence.
- Treat design-partner interviews as qualitative evidence, not proof of market traction by themselves.
